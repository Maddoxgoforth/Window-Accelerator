# Window Accelerator — Offer Ladder

**Status:** Internal reference — offer design, not yet built into a site or checkout.
**Model:** Digital product (info product / cohort program), modeled on the Creator Blueprint ladder (low → mid → high ticket), adapted to window cleaning.
**Audience:** People who want to start or grow a window cleaning business — side-hustlers up through owners trying to scale past themselves.

---

## 1. The Ladder at a Glance

| | Low Ticket | Mid Ticket | High Ticket |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Window Accelerator — Starter Kit | Window Accelerator — Pro | Window Accelerator — VIP |
| **Price** | $30 one-time | $500 | $2,000 |
| **Format** | Self-serve download, lifetime access to this version | 8-week cohort program | 8-week cohort + private 1-on-1 mentorship |
| **Who it's for** | Someone testing the idea — "can I actually make money doing this?" | Someone ready to commit to building a real route/business | Someone who wants a mentor building it *with* them, with a guarantee backing it |

The ladder exists so a $30 buyer has an obvious next step at $500, and a $500 student has an obvious next step at $2,000. Each tier should feel like it's missing something the next tier up solves — not like a worse copy of it.

---

## 2. Low Ticket — Window Accelerator Starter Kit — $30

**Positioning:** *Everything you need to land your first 5–10 window cleaning jobs, without guessing.* A one-time purchase, not a subscription — low friction, low risk, proves the model works before anyone commits real money.

**What's included:**
- **Quick-Start Course (~2–3 hrs of video):** choosing your service area, the minimum equipment list to start under $300, pricing your first jobs, and landing your first 10 customers (door-to-door, flyers, Facebook Marketplace/local groups, Nextdoor).
- **Pricing Cheat Sheet:** a simple calculator/reference for quoting by pane count, story height, and residential vs. commercial.
- **Script Pack:** door-knocking script, flyer template, and a text/DM follow-up script for quote requests.
- **Basic Quote & Invoice Template:** ready to copy and send.
- **Equipment Starter List:** exactly what to buy first (and what to skip until revenue justifies it).

**What's not included (on purpose):**
- No live calls, no community, no coaching access.
- No new content added — this is a fixed, one-time snapshot. (Don't promise ongoing updates at this price; that's what makes Pro worth the jump.)
- No AI tools, no advanced systems (route density, hiring, upselling gutters/screens/solar).

**Why $30 works:** it's an impulse buy, not a decision. The goal isn't margin, it's proving the offer and building the list of buyers to move into Pro.

---

## 3. Mid Ticket — Window Accelerator Pro — $500

**Positioning:** *The full system for building a window cleaning business that runs on repeat customers and a real route — not just one-off jobs.* Built for someone past "can I make money at this" and into "I want this to be a real, scalable business." Same core curriculum and tools used in the VIP program — without the private mentorship seat.

**Format:** 8-week fixed-length cohort.

**What's included:**
- **Full course library (~12–15 hrs):** business setup basics (LLC, insurance, liability), full equipment & systems buildout, pricing and packaging (residential, commercial, recurring maintenance plans), upsells (screens, gutters, solar panel and skylight cleaning), marketing systems (Google Business Profile/reviews, door-to-door at scale, referral systems, local ads), scheduling and route density, and hiring your first W-2 or 1099 helper.
- **AI Quote & Route Generator:** a tool that turns a property's basic details (window count, stories, service type) into a ready-to-send quote and helps sequence route stops for density — the same tool used inside the VIP tier.
- **Full resource library:** contracts, recurring-service agreements, quote/invoice templates, door hanger and flyer designs, ad templates, hiring/interview scripts, and day-to-day SOPs.
- **Biweekly live group coaching calls** with direct Q&A and feedback on your pricing, routes, and marketing.
- **Private cohort community** — other students building their route at the same time.
- **Feedback on your quotes, routes, and marketing**, reviewed live during group calls.

**What's not included (on purpose):**
- No 1-on-1 coaching calls.
- No private 1-on-1 roadmap/business plan session.
- No direct 1-on-1 chat access.
- No money-back guarantee.

*Kept off this tier deliberately — these four are what make VIP worth $2,000. Don't offer them here.*

|---|---|---|
| Pay in full | $500 | $500 |
| 2-pay plan | $260 × 2 | $520 |

## 4. High Ticket — Window Accelerator VIP — $2,000

**Positioning:** *Everything in Pro, plus a mentor building the business with you.* For someone serious about turning this into a real company — not a side hustle — who wants direct access, a custom roadmap, and accountability, backed by a guarantee.

**Format:** 8-week cohort + private mentorship track.

**What's included (Pro, plus):**
- **1-on-1 coaching calls** across the program (not just group calls).
- **Private 1-on-1 roadmap session:** a custom plan for your market, pricing, and growth targets in the first 90 days.
- **Direct 1-on-1 chat access** for real-time questions between calls.
- **Performance guarantee:** land your first *[X]* paying customers within *[Y]* days of finishing the program, or get your money back. *(Set X/Y once you've validated the low/mid tiers — don't lock in guarantee terms before you have data on what's actually achievable.)*
- Everything from Pro: full curriculum, AI Quote & Route Generator, full resource library, biweekly group calls, and cohort community.

**Payment options:**

|---|---|---|
| Pay in full | $2,000 | $2,000 |
| 2-pay plan | $1,050 × 2 | $2,100 |
| 4-pay plan | $550 × 4 | $2,200 |

*(Mirrors the Pro tier's "pay in full is cheapest, spread it out and pay a bit more" structure — pick final numbers once cash-flow needs are clear.)*

## 6. Open Decisions Before This Goes Live

- **Guarantee terms for VIP** (the *[X customers in Y days]* above) need real numbers once the offer has been tested — don't promise a result you haven't seen a student hit yet.
- **Cohort cadence** — does Pro/VIP run as a rolling cohort or fixed start dates? Affects how "biweekly calls" and "8-week program" get marketed.
- **Refund policy on Starter Kit and Pro** — even without a "guarantee," decide a standard digital-product refund window (e.g., none, since it's a low-cost/instant-access digital good, is common and defensible).
- **Naming** — "Window Accelerator" is used as both the umbrella brand and the VIP tier's parent name here; confirm whether the brand name should stay separate from the tier names once the site/funnel gets built.
